African Grill, Lounge & Events

Milton Keynes • Late-night grilled food, drinks, outdoor sports/social experience; private events optional. Proposed late opening subject to planning, licensing and lawful occupancy. A 400-guest event and ≤499 total-site operating policy remain design aspirations, not approved capacities.

Location review: Coventry, Northampton, Luton and Milton Keynes considered; Milton Keynes selected for the investor's initial site search, conditional on a suitable property and licensing route.

Decision discipline: No weddings or private events are required in the core case. Every revenue figure is gross including VAT; the model removes a simplified assumed 20% VAT before comparing operating costs. All costs are monthly except stated one-off launch investment. Forecasts are hypothetical, not independently verified demand.

01 · Trading pattern and visitor demand

Opening assumption: Tue–Thu 5pm–midnight, Friday 5pm–2am, Saturday 4pm–2am, Sunday 4pm–midnight. Rota includes preparation and closing time; extended hours would require consent. 4.33 weeks/month (approximately 26 service days).

A visitor is a visit, not a distinct customer or cover; returning guests on other days count again. A visitor is not automatically a food purchaser. The food and drinks attach rates below represent the share of total visitors buying each category. One person may buy both; private events are excluded from these visitor figures.

02 · Food, drinks and event sales

Indicative drinks sales mix (allocate 100% of drinks revenue)

Categories split the computed drinks sales; they do not create extra revenue. These percentages are hypotheses, not measured transactions. All drink prices and VAT classification require product-level review.

Friday/Saturday bars are no longer a separate hardcoded £8,000: revenue comes directly from visitors × drinks attach rate × average drinks spend, by day. The food average reflects suya/grill sharing portions and supplementary dishes, not necessarily a sit-down main course. Drinks include all purchases from food customers too: nothing is added as a separate bar-sales pot.

Event turnover is net of any direct event operating costs, but the forecast does not assume repeat events each month unless you switch them on. Event guests are separate from routine visitors. If events displace ordinary business, reduce forecast footfall accordingly.

03 · Staffing: shift-by-shift workforce plan

Two salaried operational leads are assumed, with paid hourly staff rostered to expected demand. The salaried leads cover management and kitchen oversight; they are not charged again as hourly shift staff. Flexible part-time/casual workers support weekend peaks. Staff rest, statutory entitlements, breaks and night-work requirements need a rota audit before launch. Peak-day default staffing matches the proposed leaner screenshot roughly, but is not yet a compliant or operationally tested rota.

2026 NLW for age 21+: £12.71/hourEmployer costs modelled with adjustable 18% upliftEvent crews paid separately when enabled

Shift counts show base coverage throughout service plus extra peak staff for only the specified peak hours. The general manager and head chef are not included in counts. Cleaners cover a 3-hour cleaning shift; door staff cover the peak shift. Adjust to venue size and licensing.

Manager salary £3,800/month; head chef salary £3,200/month by default. Hourly staff cover additional cooks/prep, bar, floor, cleaning and event-independent door security. Employers' NIC/pension/holiday reserve is approximated as a flat loading on gross payroll (not a statutory payroll calculation). External security contracts may attract VAT; validate quotes. The roster is a shift-capacity budget, not a guarantee of adequate cover at the busiest moments.

04 · Recurring expenses and margins

Gross sales are divided by 1.2 for modelling 20% output VAT. Ingredient and drink COGS are percentages of net-of-VAT sales. Rent, rates, service charge, utilities, repairs, insurance, marketing and security are distinct. No loan interest, corporation tax, depreciation or owner's drawings are deducted from the monthly operating result.

08 · Investor caveats and research next

Site gating: target £5k–£7k/month base rent, hard ceiling £8k; outdoor grill and garden permissions; occupancy and safe exits; transport and parking; noise survey and 2am licence feasibility, eventual 4–5am exploratory only; hospitality fit-out condition and fire/food standards. A large warehouse may fit the rent ceiling but fail the funding envelope after conversion.

Validation gates: floor plans and landlord consent; conversion quotes; 2026/27 rateable value; fire and acoustic review; wage roster; 8-week launch-demand evidence; week-by-week cash flow; investor terms and VAT/accounting review. The exact tax and payroll position should be checked by a UK accountant.

Sources: 2026 National Minimum Wage, HMRC employer thresholds, VAT on restaurant food and drinks. Specific 2026 site quotations are not assumed.
